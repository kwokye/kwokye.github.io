**Program**: National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC)  
**Role**: Student Researcher (Jan 2022 – Jul 2024)

### Overview
Explored autonomous search strategies for radioactive hotspots in hazardous environments using mobile robotic agents.

### Key Contributions
- **Particle Filtering**: Implemented robot pose estimation and radiation source probability distribution estimation via Particle Filtering.
- **Adaptive Step Pacing**: Formulated an adaptive step pacing control strategy to quickly converge toward radiation hotspots while minimizing radiation dosage.
