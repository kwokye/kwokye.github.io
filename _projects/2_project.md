**Program**: Sichuan Science and Technology Program  
**Role**: Student Researcher (Jan 2023 – Jul 2024)

### Overview
Investigated multi-modal SLAM and multi-robot cooperative localization in large-scale multi-floor urban buildings.

### Key Contributions
- **Large-Scale SLAM**: Leveraged sequences of Wi-Fi fingerprints to establish global topological constraints, drastically improving the efficiency and loop-closure reliability of large-scale map building.
- **General Graph Optimization**: Fused Wi-Fi signals with LiDAR odometry through non-linear factor graph optimization.
- **Multi-Robot Relative Localization**: Designed decentralized relative localization for multi-robot teams using odometry and inter-robot UWB ranging.
