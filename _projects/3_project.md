**Program**: Sichuan Science and Technology Program  
**Role**: Student Researcher (Jan 2021 – Dec 2022)

### Overview
Focused on overcoming LiDAR degeneracy and drift in feature-poor indoor corridors by tightly coupling UWB ranging and LiDAR measurements.

### Key Contributions
- **Graph Optimization**: Enhanced indoor positioning accuracy through factor graph optimization fusing ranging and odometry constraints.
- **Submap Loop Closure**: Designed submap-based loop closure detection to construct globally consistent 2D/3D maps for mobile robots.
