**Company**: Sichuan Fude Robot Co., Ltd.  
**Role**: SLAM Engineer Intern (Jun 2021 – Sep 2021)

### Overview
Engineered commercial autonomous indoor delivery robot systems operating in dynamic, crowded restaurant environments.

### Key Contributions
- **Automatic Docking & Charging**: Developed precision docking using LiDAR geometric feature extraction of charging stations combined with Support Vector Machines (SVM).
- **Narrow-Passage Navigation**: Tuned local planners and costmap representations to safely traverse dense obstacle fields such as restaurant chair and table legs.
- **Human-Robot Interaction (HRI)**: Built real-time facial recognition and interaction pipelines with OpenCV.
